**Goal:** a short list of the right people to talk to, so the conversations test what steps 1–4 suggest, and no part of the org gets left out.

**How to use it**

1. Read **Where to point the tour**. It pulls the stages that hurt most, the lowest-rated parts of the workflow and the groups named on high-pain friction from steps 1–4. Those are your current guesses, and the tour is how you check them.
2. In **Groups, checklist & invites**, list your work areas (teams, products or platforms) and any other groups that matter, such as experience level, time at the company or how much someone uses AI tools.
3. Add people in **Participants**. Give each person one value per group, so the coverage counts add up.
4. Set a target, then watch the **coverage matrix** and the warnings. Fill empty areas, and fix anyone missing a group value.

**Reading the totals check**

Within a work area, each group's columns should add up to that area's total, because everyone belongs to exactly one value per group. If they don't, someone is missing a value. The warning tells you where.

**Tips**

- Aim for a spread of voices, not just volume. One person from a team nobody has heard from is worth more than a fifth person from a team you already know well.
- Include people who disagree. The ratings flag items where answers were far apart.
- Keep notes about people respectful and minimal. Only the facilitators of this initiative can see names and notes.
