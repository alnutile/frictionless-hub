**Goal:** a list of the specific places where developers lose time or energy, with enough detail to compare them.

**For each friction point, capture**

- **What happens**, in plain words a developer would recognize.
- **Stage** where it shows up.
- **How often** it's hit, and how much it **hurts** (1 = minor annoyance, 5 = blocks work).
- **Who** it affects. A severe problem for a small group can matter as much as a mild one for everyone.
- Which **dimensions** of developer experience it touches. You can edit the dimension list.

**Reading the heatmap**

Darker cells show more friction for that stage and dimension. Look for stages that are dark across _several_ dimensions. Those usually point to deeper, structural problems rather than quick fixes.
