**Goal:** see the developer's journey left to right, with the problems layered on top, so stakeholders can follow it at a glance.

**How it's structured**

- **Activities** run across the top in the order a developer does them.
- **Tasks** sit under each activity. These are the steps inside it.
- **Cards** stack under each task. Each card is one of: a step in the story, a friction point, a tool, a gap, or a team affected.

**Layers**

Toggle the layers to tell different stories to different audiences. For example, leadership might only need the friction layer, while a platform team wants tools and gaps.

**Tips**

Keep cards short. If a card needs a paragraph, it probably belongs in the friction log instead.
