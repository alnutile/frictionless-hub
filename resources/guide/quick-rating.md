**Goal:** a fast, rough read on where the workflow hurts, before you invest in interviews and surveys.

**How it works**

- A facilitator sets up **groups** of workflow components and the **items** inside each group.
- Anyone signed in can rate each item from **1 (excellent)** to **5 (severe problems)**. Everyone's rating is kept, and the grid shows the average.
- Leave an item blank if you don't know. A blank is better than a guess.

**Using the results**

High averages tell you where to dig during interviews. Wide disagreement on an item is worth exploring too, because it often means different teams have very different experiences.

This activity is optional. Use it when you need a quick signal.
