**Goal:** a shared picture of how work actually moves from idea to production, and what tools it touches along the way.

**How to use it**

1. Rename, add, or remove stages until they match how *your* teams work. The defaults are only a starting point.
2. Under each stage, add the tools and systems developers touch. Mark AI tools so you can see where AI is already in the loop.
3. Use the stage notes for anything that surprised you, such as inconsistent practices between teams or tools nobody owns.

**Tips**

- Be concrete. Name the internal tool rather than writing just "CI".
- Gather this from developers, not from an architecture diagram. The diagram shows the intended path; developers know the real one.
- Expect this map to change after interviews. That's normal.
